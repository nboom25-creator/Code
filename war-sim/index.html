Theatre

A conflict model built on real order-of-battle data. Pick two countries and a scenario; the model fights the war month by month — air superiority, ground manoeuvre, sea control, industrial replacement, fuel, casualties and political will all feeding back into each other — a few thousand times, and reports the distribution of outcomes.

Force data ≈ 2025–26 · Spending: SIPRI (Apr 2026) · Warheads: FAS (2026) · Order of battle: IISS / Janes

Belligerents

The attacker must reach the defender; the defender fights at home. Which side is which changes the answer more than almost anything else in this model.
