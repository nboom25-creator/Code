🧩 Guided component assembly (auto-placed)

      
Upload each part as its own STL/OBJ/3MF and it's dropped into the right spot on the vehicle automatically — no shared origin needed. Uses the Material, units and Forward/Up axes chosen above; the main hull sets the vehicle length the others key off. Fine-tune any position afterward by dragging in the previews or editing the table.
