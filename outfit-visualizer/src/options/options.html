Google Gemini API key

Try-ons are rendered by Google's Gemini image model. The key is stored in this browser profile and is sent only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com. Get a key →

Image model

Defaults to gemini-2.5-flash-image. Google renames image models from time to time — if generation starts failing with “model not found”, look up what your key can reach and pick from the list.

Your photos

Best results come from a well-lit photo, taken straight on, with your whole outfit visible and your arms away from your body. Add a few — different lighting, standing and seated — and pick whichever suits the item.
